Our Mission & Programs

Eternal Peace Foundation a not-for-profit organization founded in June 2021 under Section 8 of the Companies Act, is dedicated to serving vulnerable communities with compassion, dignity, and sustainable impact.

 

Operating in Ranchi, Ahmedabad, Bangalore, and Pune, we focus on initiatives that address basic needs, healthcare, education, and social welfare. From providing nutritious meals and essential supplies to children and families, to supporting hospitals, promoting education, and organizing health camps, every project is designed to uplift lives and strengthen communities.

“We can’t help everyone but everyone can help someone."

STORIES OF HOPE

Project Lifeline: Freedom on Wheels

We received a heartfelt request from a woman who had been struggling with immobility for a long time. She was unable to walk or move around on her own, and even the smallest of daily tasks had become a challenge. Her words were filled with pain but also with hope, as she reached out for help.

At Eternal Peace Foundation, we believe no one should feel helpless or alone in their time of need. Without a second thought, our team came together, arranged for a wheelchair, and personally visited her home to deliver it. That simple act didn’t just give her a means to move, it gave her back her dignity, her independence, and her hope.

This is why we do what we do. Every life we touch reminds us that even a small gesture of kindness can create a world of difference.
